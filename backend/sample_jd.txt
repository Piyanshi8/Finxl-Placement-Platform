Role: Financial Analyst

Experience: 0–2 years

Job Description:

We are looking for a Financial Analyst to support financial planning,
analysis and reporting activities.

Responsibilities:

- Prepare monthly financial reports.
- Analyze revenue, expenses and profitability.
- Perform financial statement analysis.
- Prepare budgets and financial forecasts.
- Build and maintain financial models.
- Prepare MIS reports and dashboards.
- Support management with financial insights.
- Perform variance analysis.
- Analyze business data and identify financial trends.
- Present financial findings to management.

Required Skills:

- Accounting fundamentals
- Financial modeling
- Financial statement analysis
- Advanced Microsoft Excel
- Budgeting and forecasting
- Basic SQL
- Power BI or Tableau
- Analytical skills
- Communication skills

Preferred Skills:

- Knowledge of financial planning and analysis.
- Experience with financial dashboards.
- Understanding of business reporting.
